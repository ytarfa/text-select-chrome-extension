Tides

A tide is the rise and fall of sea levels caused by the Moon and the Sun. Most coasts see two high tides and two low tides each day. Spring tides, the strongest of the month, arrive near the new and full moon, while neap tides are gentler. Sailors still plan around the tide tables printed in every harbour.
